Colour theory used

Each scheme assigns hues to four theme roles — primary (sidebar, headings), action (buttons, links), highlight (accents) and, for tetradic, an information accent.

Monochromatic

Lighter and darker versions of one base colour for a consistent, subtle palette.

Analogous

Hues that sit next to each other on the wheel (±30°) for a natural, harmonious scheme.

Complementary

Hues on opposite ends of the wheel (180°), striking contrast when paired.

Split complementary

The base colour plus the two hues either side of its complement (150° and 210°): vibrant yet balanced.

Triadic

Three hues equally distributed round the wheel (120° apart): vibrant, eye-catching.

Tetradic

Four hues forming a square (90° apart): vivid contrast, but harder to balance.
